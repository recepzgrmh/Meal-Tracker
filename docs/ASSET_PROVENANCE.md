# Food asset provenance

Generated on 17 August 2026 with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool for this
case-study prototype.

These assets are used as catalog and meal thumbnails. They are **not** user
meal photos and have **not** been validated as portion-measurement references.
Any future small/regular/large portion assets must be calibrated against
weighed real-world servings before they are presented as quantification aids.

## Assets

- `assets/images/breakfast.png` — two halved eggs, half simit and white cheese
- `assets/images/chicken-salad.png` — grilled chicken salad
- `assets/images/banana-yogurt.png` — plain yogurt, banana and almonds

## Shared generation direction

- Use case: photorealistic-natural
- Intended use: mobile nutrition app meal thumbnail/detail hero
- Warm off-white limestone surface and matte white ceramic
- Square, top-down three-quarter composition with a safe rounded crop
- Soft diffused morning window light and realistic food texture
- No text, logos, watermark, hands, packaging or unrelated props
- Avoid artificial gloss, heavy filters, restaurant drama and oversaturation

Each asset used a separate subject-specific prompt while preserving the shared
composition, lighting, palette and constraints above. Source generation files
remain in the Codex generated-image store; optimized project copies are the
files listed above.
