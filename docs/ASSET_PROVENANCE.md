# Food asset provenance

Generated on 17 August 2026 with OpenAI image-generation tools for this
case-study prototype. The onboarding and portion-comparison sources were
generated by the project owner and supplied through the local Downloads
folder; only optimized WebP derivatives are committed.

These assets are used as catalog and meal thumbnails. They are **not** user
meal photos and have **not** been validated as portion-measurement references.
Any future small/regular/large portion assets must be calibrated against
weighed real-world servings before they are presented as quantification aids.

## Assets

- `assets/images/breakfast.png` — two halved eggs, half simit and white cheese
- `assets/images/chicken-salad.png` — grilled chicken salad
- `assets/images/banana-yogurt.png` — plain yogurt, banana and almonds
- `assets/images/onboarding_breakfast_hero.webp` — eggs, simit and white
  cheese; onboarding value proposition and deterministic recognition demo
- `assets/images/portion_cheese_small.webp` — relative small cheese portion
- `assets/images/portion_cheese_regular.webp` — relative regular cheese portion
- `assets/images/portion_cheese_large.webp` — relative large cheese portion

The three cheese images are visual, relative portion choices only. Their cube
counts are not calibrated mass measurements and must not be mapped to gram or
nutrition values without weighed-reference validation. The UI therefore keeps
an exact-amount path and avoids presenting these assets as measurement truth.

## Shared generation direction

- Use case: photorealistic-natural
- Intended use: mobile nutrition app meal thumbnail/detail hero
- Warm off-white limestone surface and matte white ceramic
- Square, top-down three-quarter composition with a safe rounded crop
- Soft diffused morning window light and realistic food texture
- No text, logos, watermark, hands, packaging or unrelated props
- Avoid artificial gloss, heavy filters, restaurant drama and oversaturation

Each asset used a separate subject-specific prompt while preserving the shared
composition, lighting, palette and constraints above. The originally supplied
PNG files remain outside the repository; optimized project copies are the
files listed above.

## Controlled portion references (`food-portion-references` bucket)

The reviewed portion-reference programme is a separate, source-traced track and
does **not** reuse any asset listed above. Its gram values come from published
Turkish sources (TürKomp for composition, TÜBER 2022 for standard portions),
its images are produced under a fixed capture standard, and no image is
published without a named reviewer.
